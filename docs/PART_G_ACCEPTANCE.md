## Data and compatibility

- New optional fields only: `Attempt.experimentResponses[]`, `Reassessment.scenarioId`, `ExperimentResponse.baseProbeId`. `Counterexample` (never persisted) gained `question`/`edit` and string-capable parameter values.
- Pre-G attempts keep their legacy fixed probe ids (`learning/probes.ts`, now marked legacy) and still validate exactly as before. Pre-F (engine v1) attempts are not re-diagnosed, because the models run on the current engine.
- Imports re-build every `g.*` probe and check the choice, re-execute every experiment answer, and match transfers against the deterministic transfer pool.
- `profile.evidence` still receives `EvidenceItem` records (now derived from the engine) so older report surfaces keep working.

## Honest limits

- The 14 models are authored single-rule simplifications. A real learner may hold several ideas at once, or ideas outside the taxonomy; the UI then says "None of the mix-ups we know about explains your answers… we need another example."
- `PERCEIVED_NEAR_CM = 30` is an authored assumption of the SENSOR_IS_ACTION model, not a measurement of children.
- The mission prediction asks only for the first action (plus an optional branch). With the current eight missions, 7 of the 14 ideas cannot make that first answer wrong: SENSOR_RANGE_REVERSED, STRICT_VS_INCLUSIVE, SENSOR_NULL_ZERO, LOOP_COUNT, FUNCTION_CALL_SKIPPED, VARIABLE_STALE_VALUE and MOVEMENT_VS_SENSOR_REFRESH. They are reachable only through optional investigation and later answers. Part H mission work (equality thresholds, out-of-range readings, re-assigned variables, loop-count predictions) is the natural place to close this gap.
- Nothing here measures learning gains. No human study has been run.
