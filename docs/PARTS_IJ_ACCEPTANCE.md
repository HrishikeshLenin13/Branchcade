### J2 — unit/integration coverage

- Geometry: quadrants/cardinal/parallel/tangent/origin rays; diagonal/contact/boundary/tunneling collisions; overlap rejection; floor edge/priority; finite inputs; range-null; rounding precision; fixed-clock and playback invariance.
- Interpreter: every comparator/truth table/null guard; selected branch only; nested repeat/while 0/1/N and initially false; infinite-loop/cancel/budget/depth safety; definitions versus calls; typed variables; source-linked malformed blocks; stable trace/snapshots; JSON/hash changes; no executable learner strings.
- Learning: ambiguous first error, mandatory divergent learners, correct response bypass, contradictions, bounded heuristic scores, independent evidence/no instant mastery, new transfer/no double counting, discriminating/no-candidate/engine-grounded counterexamples.
- Curriculum: parse every authored record; every reference/negative/variant; versioned judging and frozen v1; unlock/retry/resume; assistance and revealed solution cannot grant independent understanding.
- Storage: migration/corruption/duplicates/oversize/depth/unknown versions; denial/quota/atomic rollback; scoped deletion and complete erasure; temporal ordering; reload/replay; nonidentifying minimum profile.
- Team: every size/capacity; role coverage and meaningful contributions; separate predictions; handoff privacy; gates/authorized exceptions; physical/concept completion; reload/export/history.
