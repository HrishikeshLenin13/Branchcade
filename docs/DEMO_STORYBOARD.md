It does **not** inject progress or mock execution. It verifies trace facts, both changed-distance runs, transfer, durable reload and Progress, zero page exceptions and same-origin-only requests. It captures three optimized JPEGs. It is not a video recorder or an eligibility test. Repeating it overwrites its own evidence images/JSON with the new actual capture; do not substitute a stale capture for changed code.

Use a clean browser profile at 1440×1000 or a legible 1920×1080 recording region; 100% zoom is the tested capture, not a guarantee at arbitrary zoom. Turn off notifications.
